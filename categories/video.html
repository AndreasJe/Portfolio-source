Video Production

Video Production:

Here is a selection of videos that i have created from the ground up.

Passion Interview: David Wachmann

This video is from our "Passion"-side project from the 2nd semester. We had to interview a friend or family member about their passion. I teamed up with a classmate, and we met up with his friend, David. I handled the camera, lightning and editing, while he conducted the interview and planned the questions in advance.

Introduction video: Developmental sequence in small groups

During lockdown, i spent alot of time at the office with Casper from Verdens Bedste Daske Skole. He had alot of ideas for content and the sky was the limit, so we jumped from idea to idea in a bit of a rushed manner. One of them was this introduction video, that Casper had thought of. We planned out a day for filming and invited his daughter and her friends to provide us with free actors. Casper directed them according to his vision, and i tried to forfill the rest in post.

SoMe-Commercial: Nordic Schools App

During my internship, we decided to make a simple commercial for social media. I emphasized the importance of conveying your product simply for the users, and saw a great opportunity to reach potential product ambassadors on LinkedIn. A few days later, we had arranged a day for filming at Freinetskolen in Valby and i processed the video at home, and tweaked it to Caspers wishes, as i shared it with him along the process

SoMe-Commercial: Adam Vecht Tattoo

As part of our second semester exam, a classmate and i cooperated with Adam Vecht. He is a tatto artist working numerous jobs with his own shop in Ørestaden. We were invited to come see him do a full leg tattoo and do some shots, while he worked. With the resulting footage, I created this short commercial for the project. Yes, the music is horrible - but it was the only royalty riff i could find :)
