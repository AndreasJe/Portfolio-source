About me:

I’m a years old Dane and currently living in Nørrebro, Copenhagen. Here I’ve lived with my girlfriend and our two cats for the past two years. I’m a social person, a movie-lover and able to enjoy all genres of music, even though I have a very specific taste. Recently I graduated as a MultiMedia Designer from KEA, and during that study, I discovered a love for coding and because of that; I started with Web Development shortly after.
Now a few months later, I’ve already gotten confident with PHP and all its features. As a result, I can create a full web-application with an independent MySQL back-end with features like; Two Factor Authentication, Emailing service, Dynamic Product Catalogue, Multi-Lingual support and much more.

Less than two months ago, I would completely depend on WordPress plug-ins for the aforementioned features, now I can write them myself. This ability opens a whole new world of endless methods within coding languages and all the frameworks we have today - not even considered the ones to come in the near-future.
