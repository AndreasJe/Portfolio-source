Videnskab.dk

Videnskab

📌 Click the image above or follow the link below to visit website

This project was swarmed with editorial tasks at the end of its development. As a result, I was pulled in by the project manager to help with minor tweaks to the design. These changes involve redefining the headers for the theme pages, and going through each element of content to correct minor spacing tweaks. The tasks were defined by close cooperation with the news editor at Videnskab, Jonas Salomonsen. Even if I wasn't part of the team to begin with, I managed to make a large impact on the readability of the site, and ensuring satisfaction for the client.

Note: Shortly after launch the website won a design award given by Danske Mediers Design- og Innovationspris
