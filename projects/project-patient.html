Patientsikkerhed

📌 Click the image above or follow the link below to visit website

This project was based on an in-house solution, where our designers delivered a hi-def mockup of the agreed design. This mockup was presented to the client and us developers, where we could give pointers, corrections and suggestions. Utilizing the red dot as a third element throughout the design was one of my suggestions. The initial design had various design elements that would use the dot, but it was not aligned properly, had different colors, and was not really used properly throughout the design.
As for development, I was responsible for various custom blocks for their content, the header and footer.

Note: Shortly after launch the website won a design award given by Danske Mediers Design- og Innovationspris
