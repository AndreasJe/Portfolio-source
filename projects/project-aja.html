Arne Jensen Automobiler

📌 Click the image above or follow the link below to visit website

This professional project was a bit specific, as my dad was paying enormous bills for a simple hosting service with Bilinfo and their partners. His biggest wish was a simple notice box on the front page, where he could write small notices for his customers and they couldn't do it unless he paid way too much for it. They work with templates, so any changes come with a lot of charges. To complete his wish, I've created a WordPress setup using the Divi Theme by Elegant Themes.

The Divi builder makes it possible to create user roles, which restricts the users' abilities in its graphical UI. This makes the builder accessible with a few clicks, and everything can be changed live on the website. Now my dad can renew any text on the website as he wishes in seconds, including his notice-box on the home page.

Note: "Lagerliste" is a plug-in script created by the Bilinfo-partner Affinity. They have done a terrible job, so some features might still be broken when you are reading this.
