Nordic Schools

Nordic Schools

📌 Click the image above or follow the link below to visit website

After an great internship with Verdens Bedste Danske Skole, I worked with them for my exam project to help complete their online learning platform. Unlike others, this is aimed at the education system as a whole, where the school would purchase a license for the teachers. Here the teachers can rate themselves in a questionnaire and get ranked in various qualifications. In the end, the system would guide the user through podcasts, articles and videos related to their specific abilities in each qualification. The teacher would then better themselves by using the platform, and learn new tricks and teaching-methods. The administration would be able to access a overview of the teachers and their abilities. This overview helps the administration to mix and match the teachers and get efficient teams.

I helped them style the content and refresh icons, colours and various elements on the page. During the internship, I mainly focused on content creation and assisting in setting up the camera gear and streaming software for online presentations etc. Their beta service is hosted at NordicSchools.app, if you wish to take a look.

Long story short, my exam project was my take on a redesign with a goal-direct design process in mind. This involved multiple user tests and interviews to determine the correct user path. It was a hard assignment and I was under time pressure, so the site is not finished and most of my energy was focused on the user journey and creating a branding-oriented design.
