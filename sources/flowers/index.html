The world have been more and more accepting of the benefits that cannabis provides both medical and recreationally. With the evolution of medical cannabis and the new strains with almost no THC, make them legal in most of the world.
The EU accept a legal limit of 0.2% THC, while America accepts 0.3% THC. We strive to select the most potent strains under 2% THC so we avoid any legal issues almost worldwide.
